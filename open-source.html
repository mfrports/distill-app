The engines inside Distill, their licences and their source

Distill runs these programs, which are included in the app (in Distill.app/Contents/Helpers, with their licences in Contents/Resources/Engines). Our thanks to everyone who makes them.

FFmpeg (video and sound)

FFmpeg 9.0.2, used under the GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later, built for Distill without any GPL or non-free parts. The exact sources, the build script and the checksums used to build it are here:

The engines release also holds build-ffmpeg.sh and engines.env, which rebuild the same FFmpeg from these sources. You may replace the ffmpeg and ffprobe programs in your copy of Distill with your own build.

libvips (pictures)

libvips 8.18.7 as packaged by sharp-libvips 1.3.4, used under the GNU LGPL 2.1 or later, together with the libraries it lists (among them glib, libheif, librsvg, libexif and fribidi under the LGPL, and others under BSD, MIT and similar licences). Sources: github.com/libvips/libvips and github.com/lovell/sharp-libvips.

7-Zip (archives)

7-Zip 26.04 by Igor Pavlov, used under the GNU LGPL, with the unRAR licence restriction for its RAR code (it may not be used to re-create the RAR compression algorithm). Source: 7-zip.org.
